# DII public website visual references

These user-supplied images are preserved as visual inspiration for future DII public-site work. They are reference material only: they are not loaded by the current website and are not cleared for direct publication or copying.

## References

- `integrated-systems-blue-glass.png` — connected glass modules, system-flow lines, concise benefit statements, pale blue glow and generous whitespace.
- `business-solutions-purple-glass.png` — three-column product comparison, circular icon badges, lavender glass panels, dimensional illustrations and a strong CTA strip.
- `all-in-one-digital-growth-map.png` — central-device composition, surrounding service tiles, violet-to-blue atmosphere, benefit bar and contact band.

## Shared ideas to retain

- Light, atmospheric blue/lavender backgrounds rather than dark hero cards.
- Soft glass depth, restrained shadows and luminous edge highlights.
- Products shown as one connected system.
- Strong hierarchy: one clear promise, scannable supporting points and a direct CTA.
- Modular diagrams and cards that can adapt cleanly across screen sizes.

## Adaptation boundary

Translate the composition patterns into DII/Dhruvi branding and accessible Dhruvi Core components. Do not reuse third-party names, logos, contact details or artwork. Landing Page V1 remains unchanged.
